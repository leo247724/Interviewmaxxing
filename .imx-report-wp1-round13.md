## A. BambooHR (Zinda 881 = app_c4d1ec36…, 891 = app_d40d7841…): generated ids churn

Live detail: "changed while filling (appeared: #11 FabricTextField-355; removed: #11
FabricTextField-68; changed: #5 streetAddress.value (binding); #6 city.value (binding); #8
zip.value (binding))". Once a Yes/No is answered, BambooHR mounts its Fabric text fields
again under new generated ids:
- "Date Available" has no name, so its field id is its DOM id, and that id changes.
- Address, City and ZIP keep their names but get new selectors.

No required marker changed. Round 12's inferred cause for this stop (sponsorship becoming
required) was wrong; round 12's detail showed the real one.

Fix (`runtime.py`):
- `_renamed_questions`: a question matched by id on neither side is the same question when it
  is the same occurrence of the same shape after the same question matched by id. The shape
  is wording, placeholder, control and options. So it is matched by label, position and shape,
  not by id.
- `_as_fill_ids` re-keys every page the fill guard reads (`_fresh_model`, the transient wait,
  the step readback) to the ids the fill knows. The existing rebind path then treats the
  re-render as a re-render: the next write goes through the re-resolved control, and "Date
  Available" is typed into its new element.
- `_changed_since_fill` is rename-aware and compares stable signatures. A step whose ids or
  selectors churned after the fill is no change for `advance`, `submit` or `prepare_review`
  (`_filled_stable`).
- The runner still resolves once more when the packet's form fingerprint no longer matches
  the ids on the page. That converges: the second fill writes nothing, so nothing churns.

Mock `bamboohr-churn` (BWA-BH-184):
- Address, City, ZIP and an unnamed "Date Available" carry `FabricTextField-<n>` ids
  (`Field.fabric_id`, `Field.unnamed`).
- `FABRIC_JS` mounts them all again under new ids, values kept, on every Yes/No answer.
- The date comes after both Yes/No questions, so it is written after two re-renders.

## B. Greenhouse (KnowBe4, app_a17b6ba8…): the race question after "No" to Hispanic/Latino

Live detail: "appeared: #25 race; changed: #24 hispanic_ethnicity (help text)", failing at
Veteran Status.
- The inspected order was gender (#23), hispanic_ethnicity (#24), veteran_status (#25).
- Race is *inserted* right after its trigger, not appended after every approved field as the
  brief assumed.
- Round 11 would have accepted the insertion, but not the Hispanic/Latino question's
  help-text change: a question's fingerprint includes its help text.

Fix (`runtime.py` `_follow_ups`, `_QuestionDelta`): follow-up changes are taken in and the fill
goes on with the approved answers, as the brief asks. Round 11 stopped at once. Follow-up
changes are:
- unanswered questions that appear anywhere;
- right after a choice this fill made, questions that become required or optional or whose
  help text changes.

The step's readback then:
- reports each new question `SKIPPED` and named: "appeared while filling (Please identify your
  race); answered once this step is resolved again";
- asks for a fresh inspection: "1 question(s) appeared (…) and 1 question(s) (Are you
  Hispanic/Latino?) changed their help text after the answer to 'Are you Hispanic/Latino?';
  inspect this step and resolve it again before continuing".

When a choice changes the requiredness or help text of a question *not written yet* (round
12's sponsorship case), the fill still stops there for that inspection. That question's
approved answer is not written.

**Where I departed from the brief** ("help-text changes never count as changes"): the
independent C4R review's tests (`test_between_fill_mutations.py`, `test_review_c4r.py`) pin
the opposite for changes that follow a *typed* answer. A page that re-words a later question's
help text ("Enter your account recovery answer"), flips its requiredness or smuggles in a
pre-checked attestation must stop the fill before anything else is written. Both hold with
this rule:
- help-text and requiredness changes count as follow-ups only right after a choice;
- a question that appears already answered (a box checked) is a changed page.

KnowBe4's change follows the Hispanic/Latino choice, so it is covered.

Mock `greenhouse-eeo` (BWA-GH-130): native selects. `REVEAL_JS` now also takes select
triggers and a `Field.reveal_hint`, which adds help text to the trigger's own block. "No" to
Hispanic/Latino mounts the race question after it and changes that question's help text.

## C. Teamtailor (Qevlar, app_37c7cf00…): a question that appears mid-fill

Live detail: `candidate[answers_attributes][0][text]` "appeared or changed while filling; not
answered by this packet", label null.
- The stored screenshot shows it is "Linkedin profile *", the form's first question, empty.
- None of the four runs' inspections contained it (routing traces).
- The three earlier runs ended "final form needs correction" with no missing inputs: the
  browser's validity check found the empty required field that the model lacked.
- Retry six's step readback saw it appear.
- So it is not there (or not visible) at inspection and shows up during or after the fill.
- A `form=` association is ruled out: the inspector assigns controls by `el.form`.
- My best reading is lazy rendering as the form scrolls into view. The exact trigger is not
  proven.

Fix:
- Unanswered questions that appear after any write, not only after a choice, are follow-ups.
- A failure now names the question that appeared, by its wording (page text, never a value):
  - in the change summary: "appeared: #1 candidate[answers_attributes][0][text] (Linkedin
    profile)";
  - in its own result: "appeared or changed while filling (Linkedin profile); not answered
    by this packet".

Mock `teamtailor-late` (BWA-TT-172): the form sits below a 2400 px job description
(`Job.description_px`). Its first question, "Linkedin profile", is an empty placeholder that
`LAZY_JS` (an `IntersectionObserver`) renders once it scrolls into view, which happens while
the first answers are typed (`Field.lazy`).

Residual risk: if the live question stays invisible to the inspector until after the fill,
the run still ends "final form needs correction", as in the earlier runs. A read-only look at
how Teamtailor mounts its custom questions would settle it.
