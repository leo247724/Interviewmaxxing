# Candidate decision rules

The actual run scope is authoritative. These examples capture performance-marketing search lessons; other role focuses require their own semantic interpretation.

## Semantic fit

Look for responsibility for paid search/social, acquisition budgets, CAC/ROAS, conversion experiments, demand generation, lifecycle acquisition, or attributable pipeline. A broad title needs duty-level evidence. A product-marketing title can qualify when its responsibilities include paid media, SEO, ABM, and demand execution. A growth title can fail when the employee only supplies analysis, creative assets, data governance, or logistics to the actual channel owner.

| Evidence | Decision implication |
| --- | --- |
| Owns SEM budgets, paid-social testing, and conversion targets | Strong performance-marketing fit, subject to other scope checks |
| Product Marketing Specialist personally runs paid campaigns and ABM demand programs | Can fit despite a different title |
| Growth Analytics Director only builds dashboards for acquisition teams | Exclude from an operator scope unless other duties establish execution ownership |
| Senior Data Platform Engineer builds pipelines used by marketers | Not a marketing operator role |
| “Acquisition” means corporate M&A; “growth” means recruiting | Not relevant keyword evidence |
| General creative/content production, PR, partner relations, or positioning alone | Insufficient performance ownership |
| Executive/consulting role does own acquisition but requires unverified credentials | Separate qualification review; do not fabricate suitability |

## Location, work arrangement, and currentness

- An Austin-only onsite/hybrid run excludes nationwide remote and Texas-only remote alike. A later nationwide remote request is a different scope. Preserve that distinction.
- An office address, “local candidates preferred,” or optional coworking perk is not proof of required or explicitly available Austin onsite/hybrid work. Read the role's attendance language.
- Primary employer wording overrides contradictory syndicated labels. Record both observations and the resolution instead of silently rewriting the evidence.
- A recent search/index timestamp is not a posting or availability date. An employer's filled/closed notice outweighs a surviving marketplace card.
- Distinguish an expired copy from a current employer requisition. Prove the replacement identity; do not relabel a dead source ID as active.

## Pay policy

For a USD100,000/year floor (replace with the actual scope):

| Evidence | `review_uncertain` | `confirmed_floor` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employer USD110–140k/year | SAVE may qualify | SAVE may qualify |
| Employer USD80–120k/year | SAVE with offer/floor review | HOLD until sufficient evidence |
| Employer USD70–95k/year | EXCLUDE | EXCLUDE |
| Employer minimum USD90k, no maximum | SAVE with pay review; top is unknown | HOLD |
| “Competitive,” missing pay, or third-party estimate | SAVE with pay review if other constraints qualify | HOLD |
| Hourly figure without guaranteed annual hours | Uncertain annual pay; no invented conversion | HOLD |

Only employer-published, explicit bounds receive numeric compensation fields. Google estimates, JobLeads estimates, and unsourced snippets remain raw text with basis estimated or unknown. A listed base-pay range is not total compensation. Do not add speculative equity/bonus. Currency and period must be explicit; only conversions justified by the native contract and source evidence are allowed.

## Identity and deduplication

Check all tracked lanes before details and again before approval. Existing Saved, Applied, Interview, and Closed entries are protected; a repeat is not a new success. Check company aliases, recruiter reposts, title changes, location variants, and renamed requisitions across sources.

Prefer proven employer ATS keys plus source posting IDs/job-specific URL identities. The native helper supports specific URL shapes, not every site with that ATS brand. Unsupported Workday `myworkdaysite` or Salesforce careers links need explicit requisition evidence or an identity-review caveat. Never infer a canonical key from a generic application endpoint.

Normalized company/title matches are conservative holds, not permission to merge. The current helper keeps that hold even if a reviewer suspects separate requisitions. Record evidence of distinct requisitions for a supported follow-up; do not alter titles, delete old cards, or edit the ledger to bypass the hold. Keep unresolved aliases held, and use the canonical ID returned by the store rather than counting every source ID.
